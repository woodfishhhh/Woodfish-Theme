Woodfish adds only the display label and `theme.meta.json` (accent gradients and
cursor colors picked from each theme's own palette); the effects come from the
independent runtime overlay.

`Woodfish Black`, `Woodfish Neon` and `Woodfish Light` are original Woodfish
palettes under the MIT License, generated by `scripts/generate-themes.js`.

`packages/theme-core/src/palettes/ported.ts` holds palettes that
`scripts/port-palettes.js` reads out of the bundled theme bases, so other
Woodfish apps (the Codex desktop tool) can offer the same themes. Those colors
keep the licenses above: the Woodfish Dark palette is derived from Bearded
Theme Arc (GPL-3.0-only), every other ported palette from its MIT theme. Anything distributed with the
Woodfish Dark palette, such as a build of `apps/desktop-cli`, must carry the
Bearded Theme notice and comply with GPL-3.0.

The frosted-glass layer's list of workbench surfaces and its default blur and
saturation values were informed by Frosted Glass Theme by RichardLuo (MIT,
https://marketplace.visualstudio.com/items?itemName=RichardLuo.frosted-glass-theme)
and by the Fluent Design acrylic guidance. No code from it is bundled.

The Woodfish runtime overlay is independent of every bundled theme and can be
used with any active color theme.
